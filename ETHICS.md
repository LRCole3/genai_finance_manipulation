# Research ethics and artifact handling

The studies run locally with synthetic catalogue identities and investor scenarios. They do not publish promotional material, execute trades, contact providers, or involve real investor decisions. Corpus generation and model judging use local models under the current protocol.

Before generating corpora, check entity names and ticker strings against dated registry sources, record coverage, and replace collisions. Use an explicit synthetic namespace in released materials. The current harness identifiers have not yet completed this check. An incomplete search does not establish uniqueness.

Keep all licensed model/dataset terms and generator provenance with the manifests. Human annotators receive synthetic scenarios and are told the purpose and limits of labeling; record applicable institutional review requirements before recruiting participants. No categorical legal conclusion follows from research intent or fictional naming.

Release decisions are recorded separately for prompts, code, annotations, corpora and trained adapters. Publish only artifacts cleared for their license and intended research use, with clear synthetic labeling and limitations. No release or public posting is performed by this document. Results must distinguish simulation from real-model experiments and disclose null findings, exclusions and failed installation.
